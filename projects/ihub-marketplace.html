01 / The challenge

Bring iPhone accessories, repair services and custom-cover customization into one focused shopping experience.

Organize the customer journey around product discovery, service discovery, cart and checkout, with distinct vendor and administrator workflows.

02 / Features & scope

What the project explores.

  • Product catalogue and product details
  • Cart and checkout interface
  • Repair services and custom-cover concept
  • User, vendor and admin dashboard concepts
  • Vendor approval and product management flow
